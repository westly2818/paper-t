    How the bot decides
    • Before the open it reads each stock's daily chart: trend (20 and 50-day averages), RSI, daily range, liquidity. It shortlists the strongest movers it can afford.
    • It watches the first 15 minutes and marks the high and low of that opening range.
    • It buys a break above the high (or shorts a break below the low) only when a 5-minute candle closes beyond it, price is on the right side of VWAP, volume is not weak, and Nifty agrees.
    • Position size is set so that hitting the stop loses about 1% of the portfolio. Target is 2× the risk.
    • At +1× risk the stop moves to breakeven, then trails behind price. Everything is closed by 3:15 pm, and trading stops for the day if losses reach 3%.
